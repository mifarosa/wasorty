## English

**Name:** Wasorty: Water Sort Puzzle

**Subtitle:** Sort the colors into tubes

**Promotional text:**
400 levels, a new puzzle every day and a leaderboard. Sort the colors to calm music. Start playing right away, no account needed.

**Keywords:**
water sort,puzzle,color,tube,sorting game,brain,calm,relaxing,daily puzzle,offline,logic,pour

**Description:**
Wasorty is a calm water sort puzzle. Pour the mixed colors between tubes until every tube holds a single color.

HOW TO PLAY
Tap a tube, then another: the top color is poured into it. Liquid can go into an empty tube or onto the same color. A tube filled with one color gets a cap. Cap or empty them all to win the level.

400 LEVELS
It starts easy and gets harder. New rules are introduced one at a time, so nothing comes as a surprise:
• One-way tubes
• Locked tubes and keys
• Cracked and icy tubes
• Half-size tubes
• Oil and water
• Hidden colors
• Giant boards with up to 16 colors and 18 tubes

A NEW PUZZLE EVERY DAY
The daily puzzle is the same for everyone, and its rules change every day. Your moves and time turn into points, and you compete with other players on the daily leaderboard. Keep your streak going.

STARS
Earn 1 to 3 stars on every level. Fewer moves earn more stars.

CALM AND BEAUTIFUL
• Three themes: Classic Lab, Neon Night, Cocktail Bar
• Soft sounds and relaxing music, both can be turned off in the settings
• A symbol on every color for players who find colors hard to tell apart
• Light vibration feedback

AN ACCOUNT IS OPTIONAL
Play without signing in. If you sign in with Google or Apple, your progress follows you across devices and you appear on the leaderboard. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

ADS
The game is free. Ads only appear if you choose them: watch a short ad for hints, undos or an extra tube. There are no forced ads.

Works offline.

Privacy policy: https://mifarosa.com/wasorty/privacy.html
